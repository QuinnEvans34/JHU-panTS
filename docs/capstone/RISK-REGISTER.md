# PROWL risk register

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Quinton Evans  
**Review cadence:** At least weekly and at every milestone gate

Probability and impact are qualitative: Low, Medium, or High. A risk becomes an issue when its trigger
occurs; at that point the contingency is executed and the master plan is updated.
